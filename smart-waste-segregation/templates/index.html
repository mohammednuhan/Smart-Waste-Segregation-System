Waste Categories

Five Categories, Zero Confusion

Aligned with CPCB Solid Waste Management Rules 2016 — every item is classified into one of five clearly defined categories.

Green Bin

Organic / Wet Waste

Food scraps, peels, tea leaves and garden waste. Compost it to return nutrients to the soil.

Blue Bin

Recyclable / Dry Waste

Paper, plastic, glass and metal. Clean it and send it for recycling — it can live again.

Special Disposal

Hazardous Waste

Batteries, e-waste, medicines and chemicals. Never mix with regular waste — use collection points.

Handle with Care

Domestic Hazardous

Cooking oil, detergents, insect repellents. Everyday items that still need safe disposal.

Construction

Construction & Debris

Bricks, concrete, wood and tiles. Much of this can be crushed and reused in new builds.
