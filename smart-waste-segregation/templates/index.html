The 3 R's of Waste Management

Reduce

Minimize waste at source. Use less, waste less.

  • Use cloth bags instead of plastic
  • Buy only what you need
  • Use digital documents

Reuse

Use items multiple times before discarding.

  • Reuse glass jars for storage
  • Donate old clothes and books
  • Use old cloth as rags

Recycle

Convert waste into new useful products.

  • Recycle paper to save trees
  • Recycle plastic bottles
  • Compost organic waste
