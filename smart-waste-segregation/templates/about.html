About This Project

Smart Waste Segregation & Recycling Recommendation System

Project Overview

Subject: Conservation of Natural Resources

Year: 2026

Type: Web Application

Technologies: Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML/CSS/JS

Target Users: General public, households

Objective

To develop an AI-powered system that helps users properly classify waste into appropriate categories (wet/organic, dry/recyclable, hazardous) and provides recommendations for proper disposal and recycling.

Problem Statement

India generates approximately 62 million tonnes of waste annually, and only about 75-80% gets collected. Improper waste segregation is a major issue.

  • Environmental pollution and groundwater contamination
  • Health hazards from burning waste
  • Greenhouse gas emissions from landfills
  • Marine life affected by plastic pollution
